About Me

I am a Senior Software Quality Assurance Engineer passionate about improving software quality, identifying defects, and ensuring applications work as expected.

My professional experience includes functional, regression, smoke, sanity, and exploratory testing, along with API testing, performance testing, and automated testing using Selenium WebDriver with Java.

I have worked on healthcare and EHR applications, employee portals, ERP systems, and other web and mobile applications. My day-to-day QA activities include test case design, test execution, defect reporting, retesting, and regression testing.

I have also contributed to QA team collaboration, mentoring, and process documentation in Agile/Scrum environments. I use tools such as Jira, Azure DevOps, and Trello to support testing activities and defect tracking.

My goal is to contribute to reliable software delivery through structured testing, practical automation, continuous learning, and effective teamwork.
